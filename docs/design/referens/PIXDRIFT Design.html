PIXDRIFT
Design source · v1.0 · 2026-08-26

One design for every room.

The common design for all PIXDRIFT systems — Kansli, Ekonomi, Platform, and the product registry: RITA (tax optimisation), IRMA (agreements), BRITT (business intelligence), TYRA (tyre hotel), ALVA (guided diagnostics), SAGA (editorial), NOVA (profile), MOVA (mobility), CARINA (telephony), NORA (scheduling), MAJA (oversight), MONA (display & audio) — and everything added after them. Locked against the product facade. It merges the three diverging surfaces and resolves the nine known cracks from DESIGN-SOURCE.md. Once adopted, nothing built later invents a fourth surface.

Light only·Zero radius·No shadows·One accent·English UI
01 · Principles
Ink on paper
Near-monochrome. Warm paper background, white working surfaces, ink text. Color is information, never decoration.
Rigid geometry
Rectangles only. Zero border radius everywhere — cards, fields, buttons, toggles, badges. No shadows, no glass, no gradients.
One accent, working
Blue #1F4B8F marks focus, selection and links to act on. The primary button is black — the accent never sells, it points.
Almost no motion
One transition: border and background color, 120 ms ease-out. Nothing slides, bounces or fades in. Reduced motion disables it.

08 · Decision log
#CrackLocked decision
1IdP loginRedrawn on facade tokens: paper, surface card, zero radius, ink button, Geist, text wordmark. §06.
2Kansli task buttonInk rectangle like every Submit. The blue rounded button is removed.
3Pills (rounded-full)No pills anywhere. Toggles are joined rectangles; chips are square; IRMA progress bars are square. Zero radius is absolute.
4RedOne token: critical #8A2A33. Destructive buttons are outlined critical; blocked states use the left-edge banner. bg-red-600 and hover:text-danger are removed.
5TYRA status colorsEmerald/amber/red/zinc map to operational / development / critical / muted. Badges become dot + mono label.
6H1 scaleOne scale (§03). H1 28 everywhere; 40 tabular reserved for the money figure. text-2xl/3xl/4xl H1 mix is removed.
7Voice / languageEnglish UI throughout (owner decision 2026-08-26; supersedes “product = Swedish” in DESIGN-SOURCE §5). English stays canonical; Swedish ships as a locale via the terminology registry. Short sentences, honest about what is missing.
8MarkText wordmark PIXDRIFT (0.18em) in all chrome and on sign-in. PixelMark survives only as favicon. Blue P-square is removed.
9Hover motionAll cards and buttons: border/background color transition 120 ms ease-out. Rail stays instant. Nothing else moves.
09 · Never
— Dark theme
— Shadows, gradients, glass, glow
— Border radius above zero
— Filled red buttons or red badges
— Toasts, modals, skeleton loaders
— Icons in navigation, avatars, notification bells
— A second accent color
— Emoji in the interface
— Systems that do not exist in the catalog
— Mixed languages on one row
