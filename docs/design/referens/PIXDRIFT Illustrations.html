PIXDRIFT
Illustration system · v2 · continues Design source

Every object, drawn as a volume.

The graphic model library for all PIXDRIFT systems — the way Uber drew a model for every car. Isometric volumes in three ink tones, one accent per figure, on warm paper. Clearly animated: figures assemble, float and move. Built to be understood without reading a word, and to grow to hundreds of figures on the same rules.

01 · Grammar
2:1 projection, three tones. Every volume has a top (#363B42), a light side (#101317) and a dark side (#1C2127). Flat fills, no outlines, no gradients. The iso axes always run 2:1 — two units across, one unit down.
Ink
Accent
Neutral
Three materials. Ink for the subject, neutral stone for context (paper, ground, secondary objects), accent for exactly one thing per figure — the thing the system acts on. Nine swatches build everything.
Ground and light. Every figure stands on a soft elliptical shadow at 6 % ink — the only soft edge in the whole system. Light always comes from the upper left. Nothing else casts shadow.
Motion is part of the figure. Figures assemble on entry (parts rise in, 0.7 s staggered), then breathe (a 4.5 s float). The accent element additionally does its job: it travels, drops, pulses. Reduced motion freezes everything to the final frame.

05 · Education
Onboarding and explanation use the same figures as everywhere else: numbered scenes, one action each, accent dots streaming between them. Captions are optional — the strip must survive with them removed. Example: what the counterparty experiences in IRMA.

06 · Usage & never
Scale
Icons 24 px flat ink. Objects from 120 px. System models from 180 px. Hero scenes composed from library objects at any size — never stretch an icon into an illustration.
Composition
One accent per scene. Subjects in ink, context in neutral, ground shadow at 6 %. Scenes stand on paper — never boxed, never on colored backgrounds.
Never
Outlines, gradients, glow, perspective distortion, photoreal texture, faces with features, mascots, more than one accent, dark backgrounds, emoji.
