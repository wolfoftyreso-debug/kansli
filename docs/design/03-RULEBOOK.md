# Rulebook — anyone must understand
A person who reads no Swedish and no English still understands who does what,
with what, and how it went. Words are captions, never carriers.

1. One figure, one meaning — nothing reused decoratively.
2. State is shape plus color — the colorblind reader gets the same message from shape.
3. Everything reads left to right — input left, outcome right, time the same way.
4. One accent per view — blue marks the one thing that matters now.
5. Words are optional — if deleting the caption breaks the meaning, redraw the figure.
+ Motion shows the job — assembly/breathing/accent-work only; reduced-motion freezes complete.

The wordless sentence: someone → does something → result.
Subject from the people library, verb from the 16 work scenes (carry, clean, drive,
deliver, repair, inspect, plant, call, register, pay, sign, meet, teach, analyse,
wait, approve), outcome from the state language. Show the work TARGET, not just
the tool (cleaning shows the dirty floor and the shine behind the mop).

Badges: geometric marks for work state; expression faces ONLY for human feedback.
Never mixed on one element. Never: meaning by text/color alone · figures changing
meaning between rooms · two accents · right-to-left flows · faces on machines ·
scenes outside the libraries · decorative motion · unicode emoji.
